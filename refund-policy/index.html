1. Our approach

You are told the price of any service before you pay, and you must agree to it first. If you are charged for something you did not agree to, or we did not deliver, we will make it right.

2. No charge before you agree

You can end a call before agreeing to a paid service without being charged. [Confirm whether the initial consultation is free.]

3. When you are entitled to a full refund

  • We charged you without your agreement, or charged more than the price you agreed.
  • You were charged twice for the same service.
  • We did not deliver the service you paid for.
  • We could not begin the service for a reason on our side.
  • A refund is required by law where you live.

4. Partial refunds

If part of the agreed service was delivered and part was not, we may refund the part not delivered. If you ask for a refund because the problem was not fixed, we will review the notes of what was done and may offer a full or partial refund at our discretion [confirm policy: e.g. full refund if unresolved within X days].

5. When a refund may not apply

  • Charges by third parties, such as a provider, retailer or repair shop.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, pixels and scripts, serve similar purposes. They help a site work, remember choices, and, if you allow it, understand how it is used.

2. What we use today

TKBNet uses (a) essential browser storage: your cookie consent choice and, if you use our interactive checklists, your tick-box progress. This stays on your device and is not used for advertising. (b) A Google Ads tag (Google tag / gtag.js) that measures how our advertising campaigns lead to visits and calls.

The Google tag runs in Consent Mode. Until you choose, advertising and analytics storage are set to denied: Google’s tag does not set advertising cookies or use your data for ad personalization. Even so, when the tag loads, Google may receive limited, cookieless signals such as the page address, your browser type and IP address, and a timestamp. If you choose Accept all, the tag may set cookies to link ad clicks to visits and calls. If you choose Essential only, those cookies are not set. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

4. Call tracking

If we use call-tracking, a number shown on our site may vary based on how you reached us so that we can tell which campaign produced a call. This is used only with your permission to measure advertising. Calls to our main number always reach TKBNet at (833) 581-4309.

5. Managing your choices

  • Use the banner on your first visit, or click “Cookie settings” in the site footer at any time.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and site data. Blocking essential storage may stop some features, such as saved checklist progress.
  • Enable Global Privacy Control in your browser. We treat it as an opt-out of advertising measurement.

6. Third parties

Google LLC, through the Google tag, may set its own cookies if you allow advertising or analytics storage. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7. How long storage lasts

Your consent choice is kept on your device until you clear it or change it. We ask again after [12 months]. Checklist progress is kept until you clear your browser data.

8. Changes

We will update this policy and date when our use of cookies changes.

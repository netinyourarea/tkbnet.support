3. How we use information

  • To answer calls, troubleshoot problems and follow up on your request.
  • To take payment for agreed services and process refunds.
  • To monitor quality, train staff and resolve complaints.
  • To measure which ads and pages bring useful calls, where you have allowed analytics or advertising measurement.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that work for us, such as telephony, call-recording, remote-support, payment processing, website hosting providers (GitHub Pages) and advertising measurement (Google) [full list to be confirmed]. They may use the data only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law, court order or to protect rights, safety and prevent fraud.
  • Business transfers: if TKBNet is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.
  • With your direction: for example, if you ask us to send notes to your own email.

We do not share your information with internet providers [confirm].

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential storage by default, and optional analytics or advertising measurement only with your permission. See our Cookie Policy and use “Cookie settings” in the footer to change your choices at any time. We honor browser Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of advertising measurement where required.

6. Call recording and remote sessions

If a call is recorded or monitored, we tell you at the start and you may end the call. Some states require consent from everyone on the call. Remote sessions require your consent first, are limited to what is needed to solve the problem, and end when you or the technician end them.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards including encryption in transit, access controls and staff training. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive credentials.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states), you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold and how we use it.
  • Access a copy of it, and have inaccurate information corrected.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To make a request, call us at (833) 581-4309 or use the email on our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity, and we respond within the time required by law. You may appoint an authorized agent, and you may appeal a refusal by contacting us again.

10. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links

Our site may link to other sites, including internet providers. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post updates here with a new date. If a change is significant, we will give additional notice where required.

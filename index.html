Try it yourself first

What is happening with your internet?

Pick the closest match for quick first checks. Many problems clear up without a call.

  1. Run a speed test with a laptop plugged in by Ethernet cable.
  2. If the wired result is fine, the issue is your Wi-Fi: move closer, or reposition the router.
  3. Pause large downloads and backups, and disconnect unused devices.
  4. Restart modem and router in the correct order.
  5. Still slow on a cable? That points to the line or plan, which is a question for your provider.
  1. Check whether one device or every device drops.
  2. Forget the network on the affected device and rejoin it.
  3. Look for loose or damaged cables and reseat each connector.
  4. Check for router overheating: keep it open and ventilated.
  5. Update the router firmware and device software.
  1. Check that the router has power and its status lights are on.
  2. Look at the internet or WAN light. A warning color can mean no incoming signal.
  3. Try a wired connection to separate Wi-Fi faults from internet faults.
  4. Restart modem first, then router, waiting 2 to 3 minutes each.
  5. No signal from the line? Contact your provider, who can check the outage and line.
  1. Move the router higher and more central, out of cabinets.
  2. Keep it away from microwaves, mirrors and large metal objects.
  3. Switch weak rooms to the 2.4 GHz band for range.
  4. Add a mesh node or wired access point halfway to the dead spot.
  5. Prefer wired backhaul when you can run a cable.

Common questions

Before you call

Are you my internet provider?

No. TKBNet is an independent company. We are not an internet provider, are not affiliated with any provider, and are not an official provider support department.

Is it free to call?

We explain any fees before they apply. Please read our Disclosures and Refund Policy.

Can you fix an outage?

Outages and line faults are on the provider's side. We can help you confirm whether the problem is inside your home, and what to tell your provider.

Do you need remote access to my computer?

Only if you agree, and only when it helps. You are asked for consent first and can end the session at any time.

What should I have ready?

Your equipment nearby, a description of what is happening, and which devices are affected. Never share passwords or full card numbers unless paying an agreed fee through a secure method.

Try it yourself first

What is happening with your internet?

Pick the closest match for quick first checks. Many problems clear up without a call.

  1. Check whether every device is affected, including one on an Ethernet cable.
  2. Look at the modem or router lights. Warning colors on the internet or WAN light often point to the line or an outage.
  3. Check your provider’s app, website or outage line from your phone on mobile data.
  4. Restart modem first, then router, waiting 2 to 3 minutes each.
  5. Still down with a warning light? It is likely on the provider’s side. Report it to them with the time it started.
  1. Check the router has power and its Wi-Fi light is on.
  2. Try a cable from the router to a laptop. Works by cable: it is a Wi-Fi problem, not an outage.
  3. Restart the router and wait 2 minutes.
  4. Forget the network on one device and rejoin it.
  5. Check for a router firmware update once you are back online.
  1. Note times of each drop, since providers will ask.
  2. Reseat every cable and look for damage.
  3. Make sure equipment is not overheating: keep it open and ventilated.
  4. Test by Ethernet to separate Wi-Fi drops from line drops.
  5. Drops by cable too? Report them to your provider with your notes.
  1. Restart modem first, then router, so they reconnect cleanly.
  2. Wait 5 minutes before judging speed.
  3. Reconnect devices that are stuck: toggle Wi-Fi off and on, or restart them.
  4. Smart-home gear may need the app to re-link.
  5. Run a wired speed test and compare with your plan.

Common questions

Before you call

Can you restore my internet during an outage?

If the outage is on your provider’s network, only your provider can restore it. We can confirm whether it looks like an outage or a problem in your home, fix what is on your side, and help you report it clearly.

Are you my internet provider?

No. TKBNet is an independent company. We are not an internet provider, are not affiliated with any provider, and are not an official provider support department.

Do you support every state?

Yes. Our phone support is available to households anywhere in the United States.

Is it free to call?

We explain any fees before they apply. Please read our Disclosures and Refund Policy.

Do you need remote access to my computer?

Only if you agree, and only when it helps. You are asked for consent first and can end the session at any time.

What should I have ready?

Your equipment nearby, when the problem started, which devices are affected, and a photo of the equipment lights. Never share passwords or full card numbers unless paying an agreed fee through a secure method.

Prefer to talk it through?

Call Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm Eastern. Please know before you call: you are reaching an independent assistance service, and we will explain any fees before they apply.
